# Wrela Hub Charter

Wrela Hub exists to steward the Wrela language with clarity, accountability, and long-term integrity.
This charter defines the governance invariants and the decision rubric used for changes.

Governance:
- Final authority rests with the BDFL for language semantics, governance, and repository control.
- Routine merges are delegated to domain maintainers; escalations go to the BDFL.
- Succession exists for catastrophic absence with a designated successor.
- Authority is scoped by domain and reversible (decays with inactivity or abuse).

Decision rubric (priority order):
1. semantic clarity over feature power
2. composability over convenience
3. long-term readability over short-term ergonomics
4. concept count minimization
5. teaching cost

Rejections:
- Rejection is normal and expected.
- Rejections use a reason category: vision conflict, semantic risk, complexity budget, timing, or personal call.

Social principles:
- Power is earned through concrete impact, not popularity.
- Governance actions are observable and auditable.
- The system must make good work easier and low-signal behavior harder.
