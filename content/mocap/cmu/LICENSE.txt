The motion capture files in this directory (*.asf, *.amc) are from the CMU Graphics Lab Motion
Capture Database, http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/. They are NOT covered by this repository's MIT
licence.

The data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with
funding from NSF EIA-0196217.

CMU's terms, as published on mocap.cs.cmu.edu (read 2026-11-04):

- "This dataset of motions is free for all uses."
- "This data is free for use in research projects. You may include this data in commercially-sold
  products, but you may not resell this data directly, even in converted form."
- FAQ, "How can I use this data?": "The motion capture data may be copied, modified, or
  redistributed without permission."

So: the games may ship these motions, and this repository may redistribute the files, but nobody
may sell the data itself, as these files or converted from them.

What's here, each trimmed from the database's trial (its first line says which frames, halved
from 120 to 60 a second and renumbered from 1):

  14.asf, 14_01-jab-cross.amc, 14_01-guard.amc, 14_01-duck.amc, 14_01-lunge.amc
      subject 14 (various everyday behaviors), trial 1: boxing
  13.asf, 13_17-hook.amc
      subject 13 (various everyday behaviors), trial 17: boxing
  90.asf, 90_18-fall-back.amc, 90_16-fall-face.amc
      subject 90 (cartwheels; acrobatics; dances), trials 18 (RugPullFall) and 16 (fall on face)
