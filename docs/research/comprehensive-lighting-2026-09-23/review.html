What changed

Two-bounce static transport from the sky, local lights and emissive materials. Rough reflections gain nearby surface colors. Closed convex receivers reject sunlight leaks. Moving objects receive local lighting automatically.

All eight supported local lights can cast shadows. Per-face culling and caching reduce repeated shadow work while retaining the existing texture-memory budget.

What remains

This is a common lighting foundation, not a 10/10 quality claim. Sharp local mirrors, dynamic bounce shadows, small-opening accuracy, enclosed fog and cooked world streaming remain limitations.

Small-fixture timings vary substantially across runs. More shadowed lights can cost more. The report retains raw timings and build costs. Captures combine the full sweep with subsequent foliage and receiver corrections, identified in the report.

The baseline uses original scene geometry and the same full-resident sky occlusion. The new path includes its lighting-driven refinement. Both use the same atmosphere, camera and authored lighting. These comparison controls are not product lighting modes.
