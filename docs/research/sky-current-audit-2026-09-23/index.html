Good atmosphere. Clouds that still look constructed.

Fresh current renders against selected AAA skies and real photographs. The largest remaining gaps are repeated cloud shapes, smooth or patterned edges, limited layering, and the separation of light through the cloudscape.

Wrela output is 1440 × 900; High clouds use that resolution, Balanced clouds use 768 × 512. All current stills use full cloud redraw, so they do not establish motion quality. The reference photographs are not calibrated color targets.

1 · Give clouds different structures.
The tower is readable. Other directions repeat flattened banks. The broad tower and bank arrangements are fixed in the shader; seeds change their noise.

2 · Make boundaries and layers richer.
Growing tips, evaporating fragments, transparent veils, and overlapping high wisps need different character. Smoothness itself is not a defect.

3 · Calibrate light and preserve cost.
Twilight is improved. Cloud interiors, distant haze, and the ground still need stronger coherence. High sampling already costs more and does not remove the shape limitations.
