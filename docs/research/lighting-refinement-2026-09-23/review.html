What changed

Coarse surfaces receive smoother, visibility-tested light from up to four samples. Flat surfaces interpolate low-frequency local reflections without triangle-sized patches; glossy sky reflections and the material response remain per pixel.

Small bounded scenes use more of the existing directional shadow map. Rooms with one or two lights get twice the local-shadow resolution within the same texture budget. Compiled camera regions retain geometry and overlapping transport, and returning to a resident region reuses its lighting.

What remains

This is a common lighting foundation, not a 10/10 quality claim. Sharp local mirrors, dynamic bounce shadows, small-opening accuracy, enclosed fog and cooked world streaming remain limitations. Small shadow-map edge leaks are reduced but still visible.

GPU timing is mixed across repeat runs; Winter is broadly unchanged. Neighboring-region preparation still takes seconds. Changing lights can require sharper maps to be rendered again. The report retains every run and identifies capture revisions.

The baseline includes the previous automatic bounce system and its geometry refinement. Both versions prepare their own lighting. These comparison controls are not product lighting modes.
