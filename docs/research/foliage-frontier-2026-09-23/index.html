Foliage look development · September 23, 2026

Give every shoot a convincing volume.

Our pine needs denser local tufts, meaningful gaps, better branch transitions, and lighting that describes its shape. The compiler can preserve those qualities across distance while reducing repeated work.

The gap has three parts.

Authoring

Build masses and gaps.

Reference shoots have dense cores, wrapping needles, and exposed wood between tufts. Our repeated tiers and uniformly airy sprays need coordinated variation at shoot, branch, and crown scale.

Representation

Render the same plant.

Today's three ribbons use patterns based on average shoot dimensions. They are not projections of the explicit 3D needles. Derive the reference and cheaper products from one canonical shoot.

Lighting & scene

Make depth readable.

Improve needle orientation, wood junctions and local canopy light. Then integrate the tree with a restrained understory, litter, roots, open ground and a coherent wind field.

Rules + local art direction

Massive's placement relationships and Sucker Punch's restrained biome palettes suggest a practical authoring loop: reference-grounded recipes with deliberate local overrides.
