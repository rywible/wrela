# Hillaire atmosphere: implementation audit and bounded research direction

Research date: 2026-09-22. This audit compares Wrela's working authoring branch, based on `13e39e2`, with Sébastien Hillaire's original paper and reference implementation. It establishes neither AAA visual acceptance nor a performance lead. The eight authoring categories remain the delivery scope; sky work should improve their common lookdev and playable scene.

## Baseline and sources

Hillaire is a suitable baseline: separate transmittance, multiple-scattering, sky-view, and aerial-perspective products keep most integration independent of display resolution. His multiple-scattering approximation assumes isotropy from the second scattering order and spatially similar incident illumination. Dense media and strongly anisotropic scattering expose approximation errors; improving those cases requires reference comparison. The paper also treats ozone absorption as important near sunset. These are reasons to extend and measure our existing architecture, rather than replace it. [Original EGSR 2020 paper, §§3, 5–9](https://sebh.github.io/publications/egsr2020.pdf).

The author's [reference repository](https://github.com/sebh/UnrealEngineSkyAtmosphere) includes a volumetric path tracer and a Bruneton comparison. Source inspected at commit `183ead5bdacc701b3b626347a680a2f3cd3d4fbd`; it is a reference, not a browser benchmark. Its old hardware timings cannot establish Wrela's performance. Hillaire's [SIGGRAPH 2020 slides](https://blog.selfshadow.com/publications/s2020-shading-course/hillaire/s2020_pbs_hillaire_slides.pdf) also distinguish atmospheric multiple scattering from cloud multiple scattering and present Beer shadow maps for cloud occlusion.

## What Wrela already has

| Product | Implementation inspected | Meaning and remaining limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Optical-depth basis | `packages/compiler/src/atmosphere.ts`, `atmosphere-authoring.ts`: three spherical exponential densities; default 48×64 samples, fourth-power altitude and quadratic horizon-relative angle mappings | Weather coefficients can reuse density integrals. Reported integration bounds cover table nodes in real arithmetic; FP32 and interpolation errors remain unknown. |
| Multiple scattering | `packages/render-webgpu/src/atmosphere-multiple.wgsl`: 16×32 LUT, 32 directions, 12 segments per direction, 16-term finite isotropic feedback series | Composition/ground dependent and reused under sun/camera motion. This is an approximation, not certified full light transport. |
| Sky and aerial perspective | `packages/render-webgpu/src/atmosphere.ts`, `atmosphere-sky.wgsl`: nonlinear sky elevation; separate RGB radiance/transmission aerial volumes, 24×16×32, cubic depth mapping to 20 km | Cloud-free sky uses 16 integration segments; aerial uses eight. RGB transmission is retained. Accuracy needs independent reference images. |
| Shared illumination | `packages/render-webgpu/src/irradiance.ts`, `scene.wgsl`: nine sky irradiance coefficients; atmosphere transmission on direct sun; shared sky for water | Coherent source products exist. Sky lookup for rough reflection is still a small angular quadrature, not a complete specular prefilter. |
| Clouds | `atmosphere-sky.wgsl`: bounded 3D density field, 24 view samples, one secondary sun-density probe | A practical procedural volume. Single-probe self-shadowing, fixed layer height and distance fading remain visible approximation risks. |

Hillaire's default aerosol phase is Cornette–Shanks; our phase is Henyey–Greenstein. The latter is a legitimate simpler choice, not a correctness defect. His medium also includes a separate non-scattering ozone density. His sky mapping accounts for the depressed horizon with altitude and uses a sun-relative horizontal coordinate. [Author's medium, phase and mapping code](https://github.com/sebh/UnrealEngineSkyAtmosphere/blob/183ead5bdacc701b3b626347a680a2f3cd3d4fbd/Resources/RenderSkyCommon.hlsl).

## Highest-value gaps

1. **Cloud illumination consistency.** The initial inspected cloud source used unattenuated sunlight and a positive floor on sun elevation. This could retain direct illumination after sunset and lose sunset coloration. Feed the existing colored atmosphere transmission and diffuse transport into cloud lighting; retain the explicit self-shadow approximation. Review noon, low sun, and sun below the horizon with exposure fixed.
2. **Periodic sky and exact cache dependencies.** A clamped full-azimuth sky has a sampling seam. Repeat only the periodic sky coordinate; aerial frustum lookups must stay inside their texel-center domain. Camera orientation/viewport changes need aerial rebuilding but not world-oriented sky rebuilding; cloud drift needs sky/irradiance rebuilding but not the current cloud-free aerial product. Separate exact keys, then verify timestamps for every subset of active passes. Raising sky resolution from 256×128 to 512×256 increases sky construction texels fourfold; this is a quality trade, not an optimization.
3. **Ozone.** Our composition has molecules, aerosol and shallow mist, with no ozone absorption. Add it as an independent constituent rather than tinting the final sky. The reference Earth profile is a 30 km-wide tent centered at 25 km with RGB absorption `[0.000650, 0.001881, 0.000085]` per km. Its optical depth must affect direct sun, view transmission, diffuse scattering construction, and clouds consistently. [Author's Earth coefficients](https://github.com/sebh/UnrealEngineSkyAtmosphere/blob/183ead5bdacc701b3b626347a680a2f3cd3d4fbd/Application/SkyAtmosphereCommon.cpp). This needs a versioned table/header change and separate numerical validation; the exponential-density proof does not automatically apply to a tent profile.
4. **Closure accuracy and volume shadows.** Our finite series avoids a singular denominator but can omit substantial energy. For first contribution `a`, feedback `r < 1`, and 16 terms, the omitted fraction of the corresponding infinite closure is `r^16`: 2.815% at 0.8, 18.530% at 0.9, and 44.013% at 0.95. This is algebra for the closure, not a measured total-image error. Hillaire's reference uses an infinite geometric closure; neither method removes the isotropy approximation. [Author's LUT construction](https://github.com/sebh/UnrealEngineSkyAtmosphere/blob/183ead5bdacc701b3b626347a680a2f3cd3d4fbd/Resources/RenderSkyRayMarching.hlsl). Our clear-atmosphere integration also omits mountain/cloud visibility, so surface shadows alone cannot produce volumetric shafts.

Items 1–2 were sent directly to the environment implementation owner and implemented during this audit. Source review confirms colored cloud sun transmission, diffuse LUT contribution, periodic sky sampling with bounded aerial lookup, separate sky/aerial cache keys and appropriate timestamp boundaries. The frame buffer guard now requires all 192 globals. Cloud lighting still contains empirical ambient/forward terms; this is not a solved cloud multiple-scattering model. Final visual/performance acceptance belongs to the environment lookdev captures. The flagged render-origin issue was subsequently addressed by anchoring cloud density and layer intersections to planet-relative coordinates; hardware equivalence acceptance belongs to the environment verification run.

## Research hypotheses worth testing

| Hypothesis | Cheapest useful experiment | Evidence required to keep it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compiler-selected optical-depth sampling spends memory better than a fixed warp | Compare the existing fixed grid with a refined grid using off-node transmittance residuals, emphasizing mist and horizon rays | Lower worst-case and percentile transmission error at equal bytes; construction time/cancellation behavior recorded. No interpolation certificate until proved. |
| Feedback-aware closure improves dense haze without blindly adding orders | Record `r` and closure tail estimates; compare finite/adaptive and safely conditioned infinite closure against a converged reference | Scene-linear radiance error and hue improve in dense haze/bright-ground cases; no nonfinite output or regressions in ordinary weather. |
| A small directional residual repairs the isotropic closure's weakest cases | In an offline prototype, fit a few low-order directional coefficients to reference-minus-isotropic radiance | Held-out sun directions and aerosol parameters improve at matched memory/time; reject if error merely moves between directions. Novelty is unestablished. |
| Semantic cloud fields permit useful empty-space skipping | Compile conservative occupancy bounds for the actual authored density and compare with fixed-step clouds | Equal accepted images with fewer density evaluations and better whole-frame p95, including moving clouds. Noise density does not inherit exponential-atmosphere bounds. |

These are hypotheses, not improvements over published research. Ozone and transport consistency are established implementation work and should come first.

## Lookdev and measurement gate

Use `tools/environment-lookdev.ts` alongside the shared authoring scene. Capture fixed-exposure noon, sunset, blue hour, clear/hazy/overcast weather, a valley, a mountain silhouette and reflective water. Inspect near-horizon crops, the azimuth seam, cloud edge/interior contrast, cloud motion, reflected sky and material readability. Include an altitude sweep before claiming planetary views; the current world-Y cloud layer and horizon mapping are local-world assumptions.

Save scene-linear reference images separately from display images. Compare relative RGB RMS and absolute error with a denominator floor, horizon-region error, and fixed-display-transform color differences. Check reference convergence by increasing samples independently. Measure temporal flicker and popping during camera, sun, weather and cloud movement; a single beauty frame cannot establish stability.

Record resolution, adapter/browser, product bytes, compilation time, warm/cold updates, CPU submission, GPU median/p95, missing timings and all rebuild classes. Use matched image quality and identical trajectories. Respect the existing [Metal timestamp and paced-versus-sustained measurement findings](winter-rendering-scalability-2026-09-21.md); overlapping pass intervals are not additive. Accept additional complexity only when the playable scene looks better at a measured cost that fits the game.
