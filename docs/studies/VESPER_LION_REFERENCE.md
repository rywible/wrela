# Lion form correction — 12 September 2026

The user rejected the overall clay form as disorienting and creepy. That
assessment is supported by the actual render: detached mask, long upright neck,
overwide shoulders, humanlike arms, backward-kneed rear legs and hollow eyes.
Finishing the mane did not resolve these primary problems.

Reference images were viewed directly from these artists' public pages:

- [Jun Huang, Lion Anatomy Model](https://www.etsy.com/listing/775604710/lion-anatomy-model-16th-scale-flesh): flesh side and muscle quarter. Local study copies in `.soundstage/references/lion-anatomy/jun-huang-{side,quarter}.jpg` (filenames reflect download order; `quarter` is the flesh side view).
- [Maria Panfilova, Lion anatomy](https://www.zbrushcentral.com/t/maria-panfilova-s-personal-works/206686?page=4): skeleton side and front muscle study. Local study copies `panfilova-skeleton-side.jpg` and `panfilova-front.jpg`.

These are reference photographs/renders, not Wrela outputs or imported geometry.
No external authoring application is used. Do not distribute the reference
images as project assets; retain attribution in the local review board.

## Interpretation to exercise in clay

The scapula travels high along the ribcage, but the humeral joint is lower and
forward. The elbow lies below the breast and the radius is nearly upright in
an ordinary stance. The neck is a forward wedge merging into the withers; the
head does not balance on a long vertical stalk. The hind femur runs toward a
forward knee, the tibia runs backward to the hock, and a separate metatarsal
segment reaches the grounded toes. The cranial mass, cheek arch, muzzle and
small occupied eye sockets need to read as one head before ornament is added.

`author_vesper_lion.py` authors these relations as ordinary anatomy elements,
joints and contact chains. This is a rejected-proportion replacement study,
not a completed design. The old version remains reproducible in
`.soundstage/studies/vesper-rejected-proportions-20260912.json`.
